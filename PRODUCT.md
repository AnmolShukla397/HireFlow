# HireFlow product brief

HireFlow serves two document comparison tasks. A recruiter supplies one job description and a batch of candidate resumes, receives a ranked shortlist, and can inspect why each score was assigned. An applicant supplies a job description and their own resume, builds or improves a tailored document, drafts a cover letter, and tracks the application.

The public introduction precedes sign-in and includes an interactive 3D document study. Both workspaces share account authentication and the job-to-resume analysis service. Accounts can be granted recruiter access, applicant access, or both at creation. Workspace selection occurs immediately after sign-in; HTTP session state and mode permissions gate every workspace render and API action. Uploaded analysis files remain in the active browser session. Account credentials, named resume versions and applicant projects explicitly saved by the user persist in SQLite under that account.

Scores are estimates with explicit factors and evidence. The applicant workspace has seven tools: Start, Resume analysis, Analyze & tailor, Build, Coach, Cover letter and Applications. Start imports or pastes a resume, offers a template and target role, and opens saved versions or duplicates. Resume analysis gives a resume-only readiness estimate, per-section checks, guided rewrite approval and rescore. Analyze & tailor has three views: Report, Improve and Final resume. The report checks content and section structure; uploaded PDFs and DOCX files receive additional layout checks. A keyword map distinguishes supported terms from gaps that need evidence. Applicant suggestions spell out supported job terminology, bring already listed matching skills into the summary, and turn casual experience lines into action-led statements. Applicants can describe a real task in plain language, review an editable professional draft, confirm it, and place it in Experience or Projects. Missing-skill recommendations require a specific example. Accepted lines appear inside the selected section. The final document remains editable, and export settings include type size, line spacing, margins and section order. The builder creates a one-column draft from user-entered sections. The coach answers questions using the supplied documents; cover letters use resume evidence and remain editable. The tracker stores manually entered applications, status, notes and tailored documents. Optional server-configured AI expands coaching, cover letters and tailored line suggestions. Applicant edits must stay truthful; HireFlow does not fill in missing experience on its own.
